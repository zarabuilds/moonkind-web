Frequently asked questions

Where are my journal entries stored?

Entirely on your own device. Moonkind has no cloud account system, so there's nothing to log into and nothing stored on our servers.

I deleted the app and lost my entries — can I get them back?

Unfortunately, since entries live only on your device, uninstalling the app removes that data unless your device's iCloud backup was active at the time. We're not able to recover entries on our end, as we never had a copy of them.

Can I edit an entry after I save it?

Not currently — this is an intentional design choice, so entries stay as an honest record of that moment. You can always add a new entry for the same day if you want to add more.

Does Moonkind work on Android?

Not yet — Moonkind is currently iOS only.

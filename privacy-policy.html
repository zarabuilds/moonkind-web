Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Moonkind is a journaling app built around a simple principle: your reflections belong to you, and only you.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Every journal entry you write — your mood selections, your written reflections, your timestamps — is stored entirely on your own device, using your phone's local storage. This data is never transmitted to us, to any server, or to any third party. We have no backend database of user journal content, because none exists.

The one optional exception

During onboarding, you may optionally choose to share your email address if you'd like occasional updates about the app. This is entirely optional, is never required to use any feature of Moonkind, and can be skipped entirely. If you do provide it, it is used solely to send you occasional product updates, and never sold or shared with third parties.

What happens if you delete the app

Because your journal entries live only on your device, uninstalling Moonkind removes that data along with it, unless you have iCloud device backup enabled at the operating system level (a setting controlled by you, through Apple, not through Moonkind).

Analytics and tracking

Moonkind does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or behavioral tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

Moonkind is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app introduces optional cloud sync — this page will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@moonkindapp.com.
